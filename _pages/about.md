I am a passionate geospatial and climate data scientist dedicated to addressing the pressing challenges of climate change through data-driven insights and innovative solutions.

My research focuses on leveraging remote sensing, machine learning, and advanced GIS methodologies to analyze environmental changes, predict climate extremes, and support sustainable decision-making.

My work sits at the intersection of **climate science, hydrology and water resources, Earth system science, remote sensing, climate modeling, and weather prediction**.

## Research Interests

- Climate Science, Hydrology, and Water Resources
- Earth System Science and Remote Sensing
- Climate Modeling and Weather Prediction
- Machine Learning and AI for Environmental Applications
- Flood Modeling, Groundwater Recharge, and Hydroclimate Extremes
